# Failed Transfer Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-TRANSFER-001
**Category:** Failed Transfer
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Purpose

This policy explains how failed electronic transfers are handled.

## Common Reasons

- Insufficient funds
- Incorrect account information
- Network interruption
- Bank processing delay

## Required Information

- Customer ID
- Account ID
- Transfer amount
- Transfer date

## Resolution

If the transfer failed because of a temporary system issue, the customer should be advised to retry.

If funds were deducted but the transfer failed, the request should be escalated for manual investigation.

## Human Review
Transfers above $1,000 always require human review.
