# Wire Transfer Investigations and Recalls

## Cutoff times and expected settlement

| Wire type | Cutoff (ET) | Normal settlement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic Fedwire | 4:45 PM | Same business day, usually within 2 hours |
| Domestic, submitted after cutoff | — | Next business day |
| International SWIFT | 2:00 PM | 1 to 5 business days depending on corridor |

A domestic wire is not considered late until the next business day after the
cutoff. Customers frequently call within an hour of sending; the correct
response is to confirm the wire's status and set an accurate expectation, not to
open an investigation.

## Wire trace

A trace is a read-only inquiry to the receiving institution asking where the
funds are. It costs the customer nothing, does not move money, and may be
initiated by a support representative for any wire more than one business day
past expected settlement. Traces typically return within two business days for
domestic wires and up to ten for international.

## Recall requests

A recall asks the receiving bank to return funds already sent. Wires are final
and a recall is a request, never a guarantee. The receiving bank is under no
obligation to comply unless the funds are still unapplied.

Recall requests require **payment operations officer** approval because they
carry an indemnity obligation: the sending bank indemnifies the receiving bank
against claims arising from the return. A recall may not be submitted by a
support representative or by an automated workflow.

Recall fees are $35 domestic and $75 international. The fee is waived when the
error originated with the bank, for example a keying error by staff.

## Beneficiary detail errors

When the customer supplied an incorrect beneficiary account number and the funds
posted to a valid account belonging to someone else, the bank can only request a
return. Recovery rates fall sharply after 48 hours. Tell the customer plainly
that recovery is uncertain.

When the beneficiary name and account number disagree, most receiving banks
reject the wire automatically and funds return within three business days, less
any intermediary fees.

## Fraud-related wires

A wire the customer was tricked into sending is a scam claim under POL-SCAM-004
and additionally requires a recall attempt within one hour where possible.
Escalate to the fraud manager and the payment operations officer in parallel.
