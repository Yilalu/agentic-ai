# Personal Loan Application Underwriting Criteria

## Every credit decision is made by a person

No automated system may approve or decline a consumer loan application. An
assistant may gather the application, compute the ratios, compare them against
the criteria below, and produce a **recommendation**. A loan officer makes the
decision.

This holds regardless of amount, regardless of how clearly the application
passes or fails, and regardless of whether the recommendation favours the
customer. A decline triggers adverse-action notice obligations under Regulation
B that only a loan officer can discharge, and an approval creates a binding
credit commitment. Telling an applicant they are approved when a human has not
signed is the single most damaging error in this process.

## Product parameters

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum amount | $2,000 |
| Maximum amount | $50,000 |
| Term | 24 to 60 months |
| Rate range | 8.99% to 24.99% APR by credit tier |
| Origination fee | 1% to 5% of the amount financed |
| Prepayment penalty | None |

## Underwriting criteria

An application is **recommended for approval** when all of the following hold:

| Criterion | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Credit score | 660 or higher |
| Debt-to-income ratio after the new loan | 43% or lower |
| Verifiable annual income | $25,000 or higher |
| Continuous employment | 12 months or more |
| Delinquencies 60+ days in the last 24 months | None |
| Bankruptcy discharged | More than 24 months ago |

Debt-to-income is computed as total monthly debt service, including the proposed
new payment, divided by gross monthly income.

## Recommendation tiers

- **Recommend approval.** Every criterion is met. State the tier and indicative
  rate, and make clear the rate is indicative until a loan officer confirms it.
- **Refer.** One criterion misses by a narrow margin — a credit score of 640 to
  659, or a debt-to-income ratio of 43% to 50%. Compensating factors such as long
  tenure with the bank, substantial deposits, or a co-signer may carry it. A loan
  officer weighs them.
- **Recommend decline.** A credit score below 620, a debt-to-income ratio above
  50%, income below $25,000, or a delinquency or recent bankruptcy on file.

Never state a decline to the applicant as final. The correct phrasing is that
the application is being reviewed by a loan officer who will contact them with
the decision and, if it is unfavourable, the specific reasons.

## Information required before an application can be assessed

- The amount requested and the purpose of the loan.
- Gross annual income and how it is earned.
- Current monthly debt payments.
- Employment status and length of employment.

Missing any of these means the application cannot be assessed. Ask for what is
missing rather than assuming a value.

## Hardship on an existing loan

Requests about an *existing* loan — payment relief, deferral, due-date changes —
are servicing matters under POL-LOAN-009, not new applications, and follow that
document's approval authority instead.
