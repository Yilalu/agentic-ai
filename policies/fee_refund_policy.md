# Fee Refund Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-FEE-001
**Category:** Fee Refund
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines when customers may request a refund for banking fees.

## Eligible Fees

- Monthly maintenance fees
- ATM fees
- Transfer fees
- Overdraft fees

## Requirements

Customers must:

- Have an active account.
- Request the refund within 30 days.
- Provide the transaction or fee information.

## Approval Rules

- Refunds of $50 or less may be recommended automatically.
- Refunds greater than $50 require human approval.
- Customers with active fraud investigations cannot receive automatic recommendations.

## Notes
The system creates a recommendation only. Actual refunds are not processed.
