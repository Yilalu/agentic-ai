# Card Dispute Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-CARD-001
**Category:** Card Dispute
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Purpose

This policy explains how disputed debit and credit card transactions should be handled.

## Eligibility

A customer may dispute a transaction if:

- The transaction was unauthorized.
- The amount charged is incorrect.
- The customer was charged multiple times.
- Goods or services were not received.

## Required Information

The following information must be collected:

- Customer ID
- Transaction ID
- Date of transaction
- Reason for dispute

## Resolution

- Transactions less than or equal to $500 may be recommended for review.
- Transactions greater than $500 require human approval.
- Suspected fraud must follow the Fraud Investigation Policy.

## Notes

This application only creates recommendations. No real financial action is performed.
