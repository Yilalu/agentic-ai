# Consumer Loan Hardship and Payment Relief

## Scope

Applies to auto loans, personal loans, and home equity lines. First-lien
mortgages follow the separate investor guidelines in POL-MTG-011 and are out of
scope for front-line support.

## Relief options

| Option | Effect | Maximum | Approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Due-date change | Moves the monthly due date | Once per 12 months | Support representative |
| Payment deferral | Moves 1 to 2 payments to end of term | 2 payments per 12 months | Loan Servicing Officer |
| Forbearance | Pauses payments 3 to 6 months, interest accrues | 6 months lifetime | Loan Servicing Officer |
| Rate reduction workout | Lowers rate for a fixed term | Case by case | Loss Mitigation Committee |
| Late fee waiver | Removes one late fee | $39, once per 12 months | Support representative |

A due-date change and a single late-fee waiver are the only relief actions a
support representative may complete alone. Every other option changes the
contractual payment stream and requires the named approver.

## Eligibility for deferral or forbearance

The borrower must document a qualifying hardship: job loss, reduction in hours,
medical event, natural disaster, or death of a co-borrower or dependent. The
loan must be no more than 60 days delinquent at the time of the request, and the
borrower must have made at least three payments since origination.

Requests on loans more than 60 days delinquent route to collections, not to
servicing relief.

## Credit reporting

Deferral and forbearance are reported to credit bureaus with the appropriate
special comment code. The account is not reported as delinquent for months
covered by an approved plan, but the plan itself is visible to other lenders.
Representatives must tell the borrower this before the plan is submitted. Saying
a plan has no credit impact is inaccurate and has produced complaints.

## Required disclosures

Before submitting any relief request, confirm the borrower understands that
interest continues to accrue during deferral and forbearance, that the total
cost of the loan increases, and that missed payments become due at the end of
the term as a lump sum unless a modification is separately approved.
