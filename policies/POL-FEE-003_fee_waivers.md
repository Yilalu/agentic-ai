# Deposit Account Fee Schedule and Waiver Authority

## Fee schedule

| Fee | Amount | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overdraft item fee | $35 | Item paid into a negative balance over $5 |
| Returned item fee | $35 | Item returned unpaid |
| Extended overdraft fee | $15 | Balance negative for 5 consecutive business days |
| Monthly maintenance, Everyday Checking | $12 | Balance below $1,500 for the full cycle |
| Monthly maintenance, Premier Checking | $25 | Combined balances below $25,000 |
| Out-of-network ATM fee | $3 | Withdrawal at a non-partner ATM |
| Wire transfer, domestic outgoing | $30 | Per transfer |
| Wire transfer, international outgoing | $50 | Per transfer |
| Stop payment | $30 | Per request |

## Overdraft protections

The bank does not charge more than **three** overdraft item fees per business
day. Any fee beyond the third on a single day is charged in error and must be
refunded without requiring the customer to ask. No overdraft fee applies when
the account ends the business day overdrawn by $5 or less.

Fees assessed on transactions that were themselves fraudulent are reversed as
part of the fraud claim under POL-REGE-001 and are not counted against the
courtesy waiver limits below.

## Waiver authority

A fee refund moves money and is therefore a consequential action, but small
refunds are pre-authorized so that routine service does not stall.

| Refund amount | Who may approve |
| --- | --- |
| $50.00 or less | The automated assistant, no additional approval |
| $50.01 or more | Support supervisor |

The $50.00 automated limit in POL-AUTH-008 applies to every refund, including
corrections of fees charged in error. A correction above $50.00 is still correct
and will almost certainly be approved, but a person signs it.

A customer qualifies for a courtesy waiver when the account has been open more
than 90 days, has no more than two prior courtesy waivers in the trailing 12
months, and is not currently under a fraud or collections hold. Customers in
Premier or Private Client tiers receive one additional courtesy waiver per year.

## Recurring fee complaints

When a customer disputes the same recurring fee for a third time, the correct
resolution is a product review rather than another waiver. Offer to evaluate a
fee-free account tier and record the outcome. Repeatedly waiving the same
maintenance fee masks a product mismatch and is a poor customer outcome.
